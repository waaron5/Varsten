## Estimates are not invoices

Varsten can show opportunity estimates, but estimates do not become billable savings by themselves. Billable savings require accepted methodology and evidence.

## Proof methods

- Direct avoided cost from exact cache hits or equivalent eliminated calls.
- Holdback comparisons where a slice of traffic preserves the original path.
- Replay evidence for approved workloads where counterfactual comparison is appropriate.
- Overhead subtraction for Varsten processing or additional provider work.

## Invoice traceability

Savings records should connect to provider pricing, workload labels, route policy, optimization mechanism, and the period being billed.

## Finance posture

The fee model is based on verified savings, capped by the commercial agreement. Recommendations and changes a customer makes independently should not be treated as billable savings unless the agreement says so.
